# Shield-Insurance-Project
Business Intelligence | Power BI | Data Analytics | DAX

---

𝐏𝐫𝐨𝐣𝐞𝐜𝐭 𝐎𝐯𝐞𝐫𝐯𝐢𝐞𝐰

This project focuses on analyzing insurance business performance using Microsoft Power BI. The objective is to transform insurance data into meaningful business insights that help management understand revenue performance, customer behavior, sales channel effectiveness, and customer age-group trends.

This project was completed as part of my internship at 𝐀𝐭𝐥𝐢𝐐 𝐓𝐞𝐜𝐡𝐧𝐨𝐥𝐨𝐠𝐢𝐞𝐬.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of insurance business performance through three analytical pages: Executive Overview, Sales Mode Analysis, and Age Group Analysis.

---

# Business Problem

Insurance companies generate large amounts of data related to customers, policies, premiums, and settlements. However, analyzing this information manually makes it difficult for management to identify important business trends and opportunities.

The business needs a centralized dashboard to monitor performance and understand customer and sales-channel behavior.

● Monitor total revenue and customer growth.

● Compare offline and online sales channels.

● Identify high-performing and low-performing insurance policies.

● Understand customer distribution across age groups.

● Analyze expected settlement performance by customer age group.

● Support data-driven business decisions through interactive reporting.

𝐊𝐞𝐲 𝐈𝐧𝐬𝐢𝐠𝐡𝐭𝐬

● 𝐂𝐢𝐭𝐲 𝐏𝐞𝐫𝐟𝐨𝐫𝐦𝐚𝐧𝐜𝐞: Delhi NCR generated the highest revenue and had the highest customer base.

● 𝐒𝐚𝐥𝐞𝐬 𝐂𝐡𝐚𝐧𝐧𝐞𝐥 𝐏𝐞𝐫𝐟𝐨𝐫𝐦𝐚𝐧𝐜𝐞: Offline Agent and Offline Direct channels contributed approximately 70% of total revenue and customers, while online channels contributed approximately 30%.

● 𝐏𝐨𝐥𝐢𝐜𝐲 𝐏𝐞𝐫𝐟𝐨𝐫𝐦𝐚𝐧𝐜𝐞: POL2005HEL generated the highest revenue at approximately ₹324 million.

● 𝐂𝐮𝐬𝐭𝐨𝐦𝐞𝐫 𝐃𝐢𝐬𝐭𝐫𝐢𝐛𝐮𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧: POL4321HEL had the highest customer count at 4,434, despite generating relatively low revenue.

● 𝐀𝐠𝐞 𝐆𝐫𝐨𝐮𝐩 𝐏𝐞𝐫𝐟𝐨𝐫𝐦𝐚𝐧𝐜𝐞: Customers aged 31–40 contributed the highest revenue and customer count.

● 𝐒𝐞𝐭𝐭𝐥𝐞𝐦𝐞𝐧𝐭 𝐏𝐞𝐫𝐟𝐨𝐫𝐦𝐚𝐧𝐜𝐞: The 65+ age group had the highest expected settlement percentage at 71.95%, while the 18–24 age group had the lowest at 39.45%
